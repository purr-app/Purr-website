## Your workspace

Send requests, inspect responses, follow traces, and keep your workflow focused.

Purr adapts the workspace around what you're doing — from request to response and trace.

## Choose your layout

Use Focus Mode for a single task, or switch to horizontal and vertical splits whenever you want request and response visible together.
